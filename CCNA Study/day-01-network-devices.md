## Concept (why / how it works)

<!-- Explain it like you're teaching someone else. If you can't, rewatch that section. -->
**Client**
- A device that receives requested information from a server
- Your iPhone is a client while you watch YouTube
#### **Server**
- A device that sends information requested by a client
- YouTube is a server sending info to your iPhone
- Servers and clients can both act as one another
#### **Switch**
- A device that connects all other devices on the LAN (local area network) to one another
- It looks like a box with a bunch of ethernet ports in it
#### **Router**
- A device that connects all other devices to the network so it can travel the internet
##### **Firewall**
- A device that protects your other devices from unknown data from the internet
- It can protect the data before/after/both the data is read by the router
